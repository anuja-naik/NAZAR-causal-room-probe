## Unit tests

**Result:** 11 tests passed.

The tests cover deterministic synthetic lagged-response scoring, empirical surrogate p-value construction, Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment, invalid-input handling, SHA‑256 chain generation/link checking, detection of a modified record/link, rejection of extra unhashed fields, and rejection of non-finite JSON values.

This establishes only that these specific reference-code paths behave as tested. It does not establish that the statistical assumptions are calibrated for real traffic, that an ear can capture the relevant packets, or that a camera responds to light.

| `synthetic_null_flow` (no stimulus term is injected) | 0.056442 | 6 bins | 0.437811 | 0.437811 |

These are **synthetic-only software outputs**. The high synthetic response was created by the simulator; the null was also generated by code. They are not a camera measurement, E01 result, covered-lens E02 result, detector-accuracy estimate, or proof that any real Wi‑Fi camera will respond. With 200 surrogates, the minimum empirical p-value is `1/201`; here the responsive synthetic trace reaches that resolution limit by construction.
