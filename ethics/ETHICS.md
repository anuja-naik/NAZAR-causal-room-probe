# Ethical use

NAZAR is a defensive privacy/safety concept intended to help an authorized user inspect a privacy-sensitive space. Its purpose is not surveillance, retaliation, network investigation, or public accusation.

## Operating principles

- Use only in a room or on equipment where the operator has legitimate authority and permission to conduct the check.
- Do not access, decrypt, interfere with, or take control of a camera or network.
- Do not identify occupants or infer who owns a device.
- Treat RF and optical outputs as evidence candidates with limits, not as proof of intent or wrongdoing.
- Preserve negative, inconclusive, and control results; do not hide results that weaken the causal hypothesis.
- Do not publish personal images, raw identifiers, or unreviewed captures.
- Do not claim that a room is safe, that all cameras will be found, or that a report is legally conclusive.
- If there is an immediate privacy/safety concern, encourage the user to follow applicable local reporting/support channels rather than confront or expose a person based on an unverified model output.

The app and its safeguards are not built in this repository. These are requirements to carry into the Finale implementation and any public demonstration.
