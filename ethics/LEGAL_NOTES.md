# Legal and regulatory review notes

**Not legal advice.** The supplied NAZAR concept specifically calls for a written review of legal and ethical questions around passive radio capture. That review is not included or completed in this repository.

Before any real deployment or public demo, the team should obtain appropriate review of:

- whether and under what conditions passive 802.11 metadata capture is permitted in the relevant location;
- permission to inspect the room and test the associated devices/network;
- radio equipment, channel use, and applicable local rules for the selected ear hardware;
- notice/consent and retention requirements for any optical images or device metadata;
- how any user-facing report may be described and shared.

The supplied brief mentions BNS Section 77 and IT Act Section 66E as possible report references. This repository does not interpret those provisions, give legal advice, or claim that either applies to a particular scenario. Any legal references in a future report must be checked by a qualified reviewer and kept separate from NAZAR’s technical evidence tier.

Do not claim that a generated report is court-admissible or an official legal determination.
