# E03 — Variable-bitrate response

**Status:** planned; not run.

## Question

Does the selected camera change its encoded traffic rate when the visible scene changes under the proposed light stimulus?

## Procedure outline

Document the camera’s model, firmware/mode, configured encoding/network settings where legitimately accessible, scene, and stimulus. Record per-flow byte/frame volume while running measured light changes and a stable-scene control. Compare direction and timing without decrypting payloads.

## Record and interpretation

Record traffic-rate distribution, response lag, background variation, test conditions, and any camera setting changes. A response is model- and configuration-dependent; a negative result limits the RF pathway for that setup. E03 does not establish that every camera uses variable bitrate. No result or performance threshold is currently available.
