# E12 — Human-driven stimulus

**Status:** planned; not run.

## Question

Can a judge-controlled/manual light change be measured by the phone and used as the stimulus signal for the causal test?

## Procedure outline

Have an authorized operator change a light manually while the phone records ambient-light transitions and the ear records flow metadata. Synchronize the streams, document sensor placement and ambient variation, and compare the measured stimulus with traffic using the preregistered lag/surrogate procedure. Repeat with a stable-light/background condition.

## Interpretation

The phone should use measured transitions rather than assume the operator action occurred at an exact time. Human-driven timing may be less regular than a generated code; surrogate design and response timing must account for that. No result is available and no judge-controlled mode is implemented.
