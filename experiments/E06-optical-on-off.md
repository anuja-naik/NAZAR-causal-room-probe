# E06 — Optical flash ON/OFF differencing

**Status:** planned; not run.

## Question

Do paired flash-OFF/flash-ON images and motion compensation isolate flash-dependent candidate reflections under controlled conditions?

## Procedure outline

Capture synchronized ON/OFF pairs of known optical targets and difficult non-lens surfaces, including screws, glass, chrome, LEDs, and water where safe and appropriate. Record phone model, camera/flash settings, distance, angle, exposure, movement, and ambient conditions. Compare unaligned difference with motion-compensated difference where available.

## Interpretation

Measure whether candidates remain localized after alignment and how often difficult negatives produce candidates. This experiment validates a stage of the optical pipeline, not lens classification or hidden-camera detection as a whole. No images or measurements are included in this repository.
